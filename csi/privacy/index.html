Privacy policy draft.

Initial draft: owner and legal review is required before launch.

Information you provide

When you submit an inquiry, we collect the contact details and cleaning requirements you provide so CSI can respond. Please do not submit sensitive personal information in the form.

How information is handled

Submissions are validated by the website and sent to the configured business email using an email delivery provider. The site does not use a customer database. If FormSubmit is enabled, it retains submissions for 30 days and sends them to the configured mailbox. The business mailbox and other email providers may retain messages according to their own policies. Technical request information may be processed temporarily for security and abuse prevention.

Analytics

If enabled, Google Analytics receives limited interaction events such as starting or submitting a form and clicking contact links. Form contents are not included in these events. Analytics may use cookies and collect device or usage information. The owner must review consent requirements and provider settings before enabling analytics.

Questions and requests

For privacy questions or requests about your information, email info@csicleaningsolutions.com. The owner must confirm retention periods, service providers, applicable rights, and response procedures before launch.
